Liam: protector, lake wader, and daily visitor to Grammie

Liam joined Brooke's family in July 2020, when he was about two or three years old. After a difficult start and separation anxiety, he grew into a devoted protector—especially of the children.

He loves belly rubs, dipping just his legs into the lake on hot days, and living next door to Grammie and Grandpa. His favorite ritual may be his daily trip to Grammie's for “Biscy's.”

Meet Angelica

Animal love, turned into something you can wear.

Happy Tail Tees brings together Angelica's love for animals, playful designs, and a growing collection of shirts and handmade finds for people who understand that pets are family.

The mission adds heart without getting in the way of shopping: honest availability, friendly service, and products that make animal people smile.

Happy Tail Tees is a growing small business—not a rescue organization or animal-care authority.
